Summary — English Overview

MoM Biz Reg System SHV Case · May 13, 2026

🎯
Goal
To finalize the functional requirements and system interface design for the new Tax Incentive issuance process for registered enterprises (Special Investment Projects in Sihanoukville) within the GDT registration management system.
📌
Objective
Streamline the drafting and issuance of tax incentive letters by migrating to a three-level approval workflow and enhance automation for date calculation and status tracking.
💡
Key Takeaways
  • Workflow re-categorized into three levels (L1: Officer, L2: Chief, L3: Director General) to reflect hierarchical administrative structure.
  • IT department will implement auto-calculation of end dates based on the selected tax incentive term (e.g., 3-year term → auto-expiry Dec 31 of end year).
  • "Print Draft" buttons to be added at each level to facilitate document review before official submission.
  • "Remark" columns for manual entries and updated interface modifications for better data entry and cross-departmental collaboration.
📝
Detailed Summary
System Interface
Updates needed for the "Action" tab, including clearer "Agree" and "Signature" placeholders for the Director General. DAGA will finalize and submit revised letter templates to DICT.
Workflow Automation
The system will calculate and display expiration dates automatically based on selected years (e.g., 3-year term from 01.05.26 → auto-expiry 31.12.28).
Communication
Officers can print draft letters with automated annexes for verification. "Print Draft" buttons to be added at Level 1 and Level 2 approval screens.
Collaboration
DAGA will provide updated draft templates to DICT, which will prioritize immediate implementation of these system changes to align with GDT strategic plan.
⚡
Take-Action Plan
01
DAGA: Finalize and submit all revised letter templates and flow requirements to DICT.
02
DICT: Update the system interface, integrate the requested buttons, and enable the "detect end date" script.
03
Next Steps: Both teams to continue development immediately to align with the GDT strategic plan.

  • បញ្ជូនឯកសារទៅកម្រិត ៣
